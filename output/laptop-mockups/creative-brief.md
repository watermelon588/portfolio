## Brand findings

TripVerse is conversational trip planning with a plan, sketchbook, map and 3D route. Warm monochrome: bone #F7F6F3, ink #111111, Instrument Serif, Geist and Geist Mono. Photography carries color. The mark is an arc horizon breaking downward into a trailing stroke. Existing hero uses inline photographs within editorial typography and a tall Seoul architecture photograph.

Walkthru tests real user journeys and supplies launch evidence, SEO, AI search and security findings. Name and tagline: Walkthru; “See where strangers get stuck.” Scout is the walking bird. Silver #F4F4F5, ink #1B1B1F, muted teal #4D7274; light Geist headlines and Geist Mono annotations. Preserve the real dashboard's visual hierarchy.

Shiori's calm Nagi world resembles an anime ending sequence. Sky-white #F6F8FB, ink navy #141A26, cobalt #245CFF, Zen Maru Gothic and M PLUS 1. The actual site logo is a smiling pink pig with yellow glasses on blue. Hero: misty torii landscape and “Your anime, kept like a bookmark.” The supplied still and mascot anchor the mockup.

## Art direction and composition

Reading this as portfolio product photography for design-conscious visitors, with three distinct editorial, structural and atmospheric settings. Static image output; no application code or motion implementation needed. Design variance 8, motion 1, visual density 3.

1. TripVerse — The departure desk. Graphite laptop on pale sculpted contour steps, warm side light, mineral green rear wall, small travel paper ephemera as supporting accents. Broad, subtly diagonal composition. Use the supplied home capture inside the display and its real mark. Warm paper stays dominant inside the screen; travel photography supplies restrained color.
2. Walkthru — The evidence studio. Silver laptop on a silver-gray slab in a cool architectural studio. Teal translucent architectural plane and soft projected light, with a small freestanding Scout emblem. More frontal, slightly elevated angle than TripVerse; dashboard remains readable. No fictional report scores or fabricated marketing metrics.
3. Shiori — The blue-hour bookmark. Silver laptop on a pale blue desk with luminous curved paper/bookmark forms, soft blue-hour window shadows and one subtle cobalt edge light. A small ceramic mascot beside the laptop connects to the real logo. Nagi site hero fills the display. Dreamlike studio photography with physically convincing hardware, rather than an illustrated laptop.
