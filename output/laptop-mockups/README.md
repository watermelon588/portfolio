The reference ratio is 2126:1665 = 1.2769:1. Each result is within 0.2% of it. The generator returned the native dimensions above despite the larger dimensions requested in the prompt. These PNGs are the native generated images; no artificial upscaling was applied.

Convenient copies are saved here as tripverse-laptop.png, walkthru-laptop.png, and shiori-laptop.png.

## Visual review

- TripVerse: complete graphite laptop, believable screen perspective and keyboard, identifiable horizon mark and wordmark, original editorial hero layout, mineral green and sunlight setting, travel journal and landscape prints.
- Walkthru: complete silver laptop, recognizable bird mark and dashboard hierarchy, readable main headline, restrained teal acrylic backdrop and Scout sculpture, original source figures visible.
- Shiori: complete silver laptop, recognizable pig icon and Shiori name, readable main headline and torii artwork, distinct blue paper environment, ceramic pig mascot and bookmark detail. The laptop base has tighter right-side framing than the other two, but remains fully visible.
- No external slogans, watermarks, phones or extra screens.

The displays are AI-rendered reproductions guided by the supplied screenshots. Main headings, brand names and interface hierarchy were visually checked. Very small interface text should not be treated as a pixel-exact screenshot capture.
